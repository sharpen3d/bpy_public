aether.add_region

Add Region · experimental

bpy.ops.aether.add_region(**properties)

Add a named trim strip or decal region fitted to the selected objects.

Context: A scene with an orthographic camera; select the objects that make the strip or piece.
Effects: Appends one region to the scene list. Trim strips span the full width; a decal fits its pieces plus the margin.
Limit: Bounds are evaluated bounding boxes seen by the scene camera, not exact silhouettes. Nothing selected gives the whole capture.
Status: experimental

Properties

PropertyTypeDescription and declaration
kindEnumPropertyTrim strips repeat along U; decals are separate pieces
default: DECAL
margin_pxIntPropertyTexels added around the fitted bounds
default: 4 · min: 0 · max: 256
