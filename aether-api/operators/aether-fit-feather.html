aether.fit_feather

Fit Footprint to Geometry · current

bpy.ops.aether.fit_feather()

Refit the active layer footprint to its evaluated geometry.

Context: An active mesh layer with feather controls.
Limit: Updates captured footprint data; it does not flatten geometry or alter tile spacing.
Status: current

Operator properties

This operator declares no Aether-specific keyword properties. It may use scene, object, modifier, or editor settings instead; see its context and related settings below.

Related settings

These are configured on the scene or object; they are not passed to the operator as keyword arguments.

Object

  • aether_feather — Per-object coverage and footprint controls for authored layer materials

FeatherSettings

  • center — Center of the captured footprint in its selected coordinate frame
  • size — Dimensions of the captured footprint in its selected coordinate frame
  • coordinates — Coordinate source used to measure the feather footprint
