Camera textures in Eevee

I think I might be cooking. Blender realtime camera texture in Eevee - fully internal, no hacks,branched custom build to make this happen

What the clip shows

A camera view is being used as a texture inside Eevee. The scene includes surfaces displaying the camera output, so moving through the scene makes the relationship visible.

This is a custom Blender build experiment, rather than a claim that the same setup works in an ordinary release. The short recording shows the result; it is not a complete setup tutorial.
