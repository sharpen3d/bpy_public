I Tried to Fix Blender’s FBX Export and Lost My Mind

The Issue (As I See It): - Blender cannot use Autodesk’s FBX SDK due to GPL license incompatibility. - Blender’s exporter is a reverse-engineered C implementation. - FBX exports are always tagged as Y-Up in metadata—even if you select Z-Up. - Blender bakes transforms into bones/meshes instead of relying on clean FBX metadata. - Armature space is used directly; this results in a phantom -90° X rotation baked into the root .
