Procedural effects

Geometry Nodes, Python, and sprite output

An earlier Blender tool for particle effects and sprite sequences. I built the Python controls and editable node groups for spawning, motion, scale, rotation, color, and looping output. I’m showing it here as a procedural study; it isn’t a current extension release.
